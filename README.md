# Rabbit Hole - Rabbit Hole - Retrace your tabs

Just like Alice tumbling into Wonderland, one search easily turns into twenty open tabs. Rabbit Hole automatically maps how you got there, quietly tracking your branching trails.

## Uses
  - Primary: Going down a rabbit hole and you want to know just how you ended up to where you were. Wasn't I just looking something up about fruit? Where did dogs come from?
  - Being able to easily sort through your histories without gmail, discord, google images, and more cluttering it up
  - Not needing to rely on the 'back button' which regularly when you sign in to a page won't take you back to where you need to go or even take you back too far
  - Online shopping significantly more organized, If you are thinking between a few deals and don't want to remember what you saw, this does it for you
  - When you start on a difficult topic and have to branch down into simpler prereq concepts just to understand the original page
    
## Features
- Being able to see your tab history for a specific tab
- Option to hide tabs/burrows from being analyzed 
- Deleting a middle parent between a parent and a child will have the child inherit the parent's information so the hole doesn't fall apart
- Being able to put tabs to sleep
- Seeing how much time has been since you last accessed the tab
- Being able to click the tab from the history and being instantly taken to it

## Modes
- Tab mode where you can see all of the tabs you have vertically and hovering enables seeing its history
- Burrow mode where you can see and scroll to all your your burrows



## Technical Stack

- **Languages:** Javascript, HTML, CSS

## Author's Note

Rabbit Hole was an idea I had in the middle of doing a project for CS168, VLSI design. In that class, to do our labs, we have to go over far too many files to ensure every chip is designed correctly. This involves:
- Going over the principal GitHub for the assignment
- Looking at and zooming in on the reference images, which somehow opens another tab
- Going to your email to message the TAs for help, only for their reply to be another link you have to keep open
- Going on Discord and asking your classmates

I'd regularly have over 15 tabs open that I needed to keep track of, on top of all the other tabs for every other class when I decided to switch out of it. I wanted to make something for people like me who are spread too thin, where your regular search history is too crowded with irrelevant things to help you retrace your steps. When the hackathon ends, no matter what the results are, I'll keep working on and using this extension in my day-to-day life.
